Everything lives in this browser only. Back up every few days. To combine both phones: back up on one, send the file to the other, then tap “Restore or merge” there.
If a phone is lost or broken
  1. Find your latest backup file: in the Files app, or in the chat or email you sent it to.
  2. On the new phone, open the app's web address in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open the app from the Home Screen, go to Settings and tap “Restore or merge a backup”.
  4. Choose the file. Expenses, cash, settings and photos all come back.
  5. If the other phone is fine, back it up too and merge that file: it fills in anything logged since.

Best habit: each time you back up, send the file to each other. Then each phone also holds a copy of the other's.
